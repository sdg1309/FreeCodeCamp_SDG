Spaggeti

Easy to do for a meal

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil the water and add the salt
  2. Add the pastas
  3. Wait untill coked to the desire texture
  4. Drain the water, and add the milk and butter
  5. Plate and add the cheese
